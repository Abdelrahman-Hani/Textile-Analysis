# 📊 Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales performance and profitability within a textile retail business using **Power BI**.

The dashboard transforms raw business data into meaningful insights, helping understand product performance, material profitability, supplier contributions, discount strategies, and geographical supplier distribution.

The main objective is to support better business decisions through interactive data visualization and KPI monitoring.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify the top-performing and lowest-performing products.
- Evaluate material sales and profitability.
- Compare supplier profitability and discount percentages.
- Analyze discount distribution across products and materials.
- Explore sales performance across seasons and product categories.
- Understand the geographical distribution of suppliers.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization.
- **DAX** – Calculated measures and KPIs.
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation.
- **Data Modeling** – Organizing data for analysis.
- **Business Intelligence** – Extracting actionable business insights.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 1. Sales & Profit Performance
- Total sales reached 201K.
- Total profit reached 23K.
- Overall profit margin was approximately 11.44%.

### 2. Product Performance
- Saree recorded the highest sales value at 22K.
- Saree generated a total profit of 1,890.

### 3. Material Performance
- Cotton recorded the highest sales value at 51K.
- Cotton generated the highest total profit among the analyzed materials at 7.1K.
- Its approximate profit margin was 13.92%.

### 4. Supplier Performance
- Sri Velavan Textiles recorded the highest total profit at 1,320.
- Ravi Textiles followed closely with 1,310.
- Rajesh Textiles recorded the highest supplier discount percentage at 30%.

### 5. Discount Analysis
- Saree recorded the highest total discount value among products at 5.8K.
- Cotton recorded the highest total discount value among materials at 11K.

### 6. Seasonal & Category Analysis
- Winter recorded sales of 24K.
- Summer recorded sales of 23K.
- The difference between them was approximately 1K.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Monitor profit margins across products and materials.
- Review discount strategies to balance sales performance and profitability.
- Evaluate suppliers based on profitability, costs, and discount rates.
- Improve inventory planning using actual quantity sold data.
- Separate seasonal, occasion-based, and usage-based product categories for more accurate analysis.

## ⚠️ Data Limitations

- The dataset does not include confirmed quantities sold, so sales value should not be interpreted as sales volume or actual demand.
- Profit calculations reflect the difference between sale price and cost price, excluding expenses not included in the dataset.
- Seasonal and usage categories require further classification for accurate seasonal comparisons.
- The reported discount percentage may differ depending on whether it is calculated as a weighted overall discount or an average of row-level discount percentages.
